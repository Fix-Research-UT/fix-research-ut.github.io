Andrew Fix is an assistant professor in the Fariborz Maseeh Department of Civil, Architectural, and Environmental Engineering at UT Austin, with a courtesy appointment in the Walker Department of Mechanical Engineering Graduate Studies Committee. He is a member of the Building Energy and Environments (BEE) faculty group, his primary affiliation, and is also affiliated with Environmental and Water Resources Engineering (EWRE) and Sustainable Systems (SuS). In mechanical engineering, he is part of Thermal and Fluid Systems (TFS). His lab is located in the Center for Energy and Environmental Resources (CEER).

Before joining UT, Fix was a postdoctoral faculty member of the Center for Environmental Energy Engineering at the University of Maryland, College Park, working under the guidance of Prof. Yunho Hwang and Prof. Reinhard Radermacher. He received his PhD in Mechanical Engineering from Purdue University in December 2023 under the guidance of Prof. David Warsinger and Prof. Jim Braun, and earned his BS in Mechanical Engineering (Summa Cum Laude) from the University of Tulsa in 2019.

He was a 2023 Dean’s Teaching Fellow at Purdue, where he taught undergraduate thermodynamics, and was previously supported by the Department of Energy IBUILD Fellowship and the Laura Winkelman-Davidson Fellowship.
